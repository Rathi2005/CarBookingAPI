Drive Owner — Owner Operated Car Trip Management PWA
Project Overview

Build a professional mobile-first Progressive Web Application (PWA) designed specifically for small fleet owners who personally manage their vehicles and trips.

The application is not a public car rental marketplace.

It is an internal fleet business management system that helps vehicle owners digitize their daily operations.
