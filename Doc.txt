Project Name
Drive Owner — Owner Operated Car Trip Management PWA
1. Product Vision

Build a professional mobile-first Progressive Web Application (PWA) designed specifically for small fleet owners who personally manage their vehicles and trips.

The application is not a public car rental marketplace.

It is an internal business management system for a vehicle owner who wants to digitize daily operations.

Example user:

A person owns 2–10 cars and manages:

customer bookings
trip details
vehicle availability
earnings
reports
operational history

The current manual process is:

Customer calls owner

        ↓

Owner notes trip details manually

        ↓

Assigns vehicle

        ↓

Tracks payment manually

        ↓

Calculates monthly earnings manually

Drive Owner converts this into:

Customer Request

        ↓

Create Digital Trip

        ↓

Assign Vehicle

        ↓

Track Status

        ↓

Generate Reports

        ↓

Analyze Business Performance

Important Instruction For Future Development

When continuing development:

Maintain the current Stitch-inspired design language.
Do not create simple admin dashboards.
Every page should feel like a premium SaaS fleet management application.
Every new module should match existing UI/UX.
Keep scalability in mind because future versions may include:
multiple owners
drivers
payments
GPS tracking
customer booking
analytics
fleet expansion

The current objective is to build a production-quality foundation, not just a prototype.
